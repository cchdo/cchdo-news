[GO-SHIP](https://tos.org/ocean-observing-team-award-go-ship) has received [**The Oceanography Society’s 2026 Ocean Observing Team Award**](https://tos.org/ocean-observing-team-award), “for innovation and excellence in sustained ocean observing for scientific and practical applications." This award recognizes “groundbreaking and sustained contributions to ocean observing that have transformed scientific understanding of the global ocean and delivered profound societal benefits."

As part of the GO-SHIP community, CCHDO is proud to be associated with this achievement, which reflects the collective efforts of our international team.
