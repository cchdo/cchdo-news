title: P04 Preliminary Data
date: "2026-08-11"
---

US GO-SHIP completed an occupation of the P04 line between 11 May - 05 July 2026 aboard the R/V Marcus G. Langseth. 112 total stations were occupied and measurements include reference quality CTD and discrete salinity, oxygen, nutrients, dissolved inorganic carbon, total alkalinity, pH, and transient tracers (CFC-11, CFC-12, and SF₆).
Bottle data are now available in several standard CCHDO formats which can be accessed on the [cruise page (33H320260511)][1].
